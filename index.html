Our Story

Step Into a World
of Flavour & Beauty

La Vie En Rose is more than a restaurant — it's a feeling. Nestled in Bole Bulbula, Addis Ababa, we've crafted a warm, inviting space where freshly cooked dishes meet handcrafted desserts, artisan drinks, and a passion for quality that shows in every bite.

From our fresh pasta and tender grilled mains to our signature handcrafted desserts baked in-house — every item on our menu is made to satisfy any craving and create a memory worth returning for.

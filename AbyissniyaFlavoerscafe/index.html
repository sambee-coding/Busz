Full Menu

Every Dish, Every
Craving Covered

Doro Wat
Slow-cooked chicken drumstick and egg in a rich berbere-spiced sauce.
Special Tibs
Sautéed beef or lamb with rosemary, onion, and berbere. Served with injera.
Kitfo
Minced lean beef with mitmita and niter kibbeh. Raw, medium, or well-done.
Dulet
Minced tripe, liver, and lean beef stir-fried with herbs and spiced butter.
Nile Fish Tibs
Fresh Nile perch from Lake Tana pan-fried with garlic, lemon and chili.
Firfir with Egg
Torn injera in spiced berbere sauce, topped with a perfectly fried egg.
Yetsom Beyaynetu
A generous full-plate selection of fasting dishes — misir, gomen, shiro, tikil gomen, and ful.
Misir Wot
Red lentil stew slow-cooked in berbere and onion. Rich and deeply flavoured.
Shiro Wot
Roasted chickpea powder stew with berbere. Smooth, comforting, and satisfying.
Gomen Wot
Collard greens with garlic and ginger in light spiced broth. Served with injera.
Ful
Fava beans with tomato, jalapeño, and lemon. Served with fresh injera or bread.
Tikil Gomen
Cabbage and carrot stir-fry with turmeric and garlic. Light and flavourful.
Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony
Three rounds of freshly roasted and brewed coffee with popcorn. The full ceremony.
Macchiato
Rich espresso with a dash of steamed milk foam. The Ethiopian morning ritual.
Fresh Layer Juice
Avocado, mango, and papaya layered beautifully. Bahir Dar's classic drink.
Tej — Honey Wine
Traditional Ethiopian honey mead served in a traditional berele glass. Sweet and mellow.
Fresh Juice
Avocado, mango, or papaya — pressed fresh daily with local produce.
Spiced Tea
Black tea brewed with ginger, cardamom, and cinnamon. Warm and aromatic.
All-You-Can-Eat Lunch

All-You-Can-Eat Dinner

Fasting Buffet
